Bible chapter counts for reading plans

1,189 chapters, 66 books, two editions. Compare reading assignments before choosing a pace.

One chapter is not a fixed amount of reading. This reference provides chapter-level counts from the King James Version (KJV) and simplified Chinese Union Version (CUV), for readers, group leaders and people building reading-plan tools.

Download the complete chapter-count CSV. It includes counts and fixed-rate estimates, with no account required. A small offline Python calculator lets you substitute a reading rate. Download both files into the same folder, then run python estimate.py --book Ruth --chapter 2 --wpm 200 with Python 3.10 or newer.

Make a planning estimate

Divide the count by a rate in the same unit. Ruth 2 has 763 KJV words: at a personally chosen 200 words per minute, 763 / 200 = 3.815 minutes, or about 3.8 minutes. Ruth 3 has 541 words, about 2.7 minutes at that rate.

Use your own timed reading to choose a rate, and leave separate time for discussion, reflection and breaks. These estimates do not measure understanding or spiritual growth. They are not validated predictions for children, oral reading or individual readers.

Counting rules and scope

  • KJV words are whitespace-separated tokens in verse text. A token containing punctuation remains one token.
  • CUV characters are Unicode letters and numbers in verse text. Spaces, punctuation and symbols are excluded.
  • Verse rows are grouped by book and chapter. Editorial headings outside those rows are not counted.
  • This is the 66-book arrangement shared by these files, not every Christian canon. Edition and segmentation differences can change counts.

Totals: 789,634 KJV tokens and 930,059 CUV letter/number characters. The CSV includes book names, chapter numbers, both counts and estimates rounded to one decimal.

About the supplied time columns

The KJV estimate divides by 238 words/minute, an adult English non-fiction silent-reading average in Brysbaert's 2019 reading-rate review. It is a baseline, not a Bible-specific experiment.

The CUV estimate divides by 390 characters/minute. That is our conversion of the review's Chinese rate of 260 word-units/minute using its rough 1.5 characters/word rule; 390 is not printed in Table 5. The conversion and individual variation limit precision.
